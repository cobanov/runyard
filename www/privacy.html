Privacy

runyard's iPhone and Android apps, its web UI and this website. November 6, 2026.

runyard is software you run on your own server. Nothing in it sends your data to us or to anyone else: no accounts with us, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

The apps

The iPhone and Android apps talk only to the runyard server whose address you type in. The address and your sign-in session stay on the phone (the iPhone keychain, the Android app's private storage) and are deleted when you choose "Change server" or remove the app.

"Try the demo" runs entirely on the phone with made-up training runs; it makes no network requests.

If you install the iPhone beta through TestFlight, Apple handles the crash reports and feedback you choose to send, under Apple's privacy policy.

Your server

Runs, metrics, logs, samples and notifications live in the server and database you run. Notifications go only to the channels you configure (ntfy, Slack, Discord, Telegram, a webhook or web push).

This website

runyard.cobanov.dev sets no cookies. This site, and not the apps, the web UI or the live demo, counts visits with OpenAnalytics, which runs on my own server. It keeps a random visitor ID in your browser's localStorage and records the pages you view, the labels of links and buttons you click (never what you type), the referring site, your screen size, browser and device type, and the country and city looked up from your IP address. The IP address itself is not saved.

The live demo runs in your browser with made-up data. Cloudflare serves the site, may keep the usual request logs, and counts page views with Web Analytics, without cookies.
